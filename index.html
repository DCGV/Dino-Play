How to play

Moving. Tap the ground and David walks there (a footprint marks the spot). Tap a dinosaur to say hello. One finger drag turns the view, pinch zooms, two fingers slide the view, and the target button re-centres on David. On a laptop: arrow keys walk, Q/E turn, Space uses things.

Knowing what to do. The picture strip at the top always shows the job right now, and the three dots show how many of the island's three rounds are done. A bouncing hand points at what to tap, Rex points too, and the narrator says it out loud. When David is close to something useful, a big round button appears: press it to use that thing.

Egg Beach: tap each egg three times to warm it until it hatches.
Fern Swamp: tap a fruit to pick it up, then tap a hungry baby. The orange dots over a baby show how many fruits it still wants.
Sandy Hills: tap a baby Triceratops so it follows David, then tap the grown-up whose footprints have the same colour and shape.
Warm Rocks: watch the stones glow in order (also shown in the strip), then step on them in that order.
Star Night: pick up a star, then tap a glowing lamp to put it there. When all lamps of the round are lit, the sleepy dino walks to bed.

After three rounds a boat appears at the beach: tap it to sail to the next island. Small sand mounds hide fossils: tap one three times to dig. Stickers go into the book (top right).

No way to lose. A wrong move only makes a soft "boop". If nothing happens for 10 seconds a glow shows the next thing; after 20 seconds a glowing trail shows the answer.

Rex can't show the island on this device

This game draws its island with 3D graphics (called WebGL), and this browser has them switched off or doesn't support them.

Grown-ups: try the newest Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and make sure "hardware acceleration" is turned on in the browser settings. Then open this file again.
